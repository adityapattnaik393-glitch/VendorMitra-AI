# Digital business profiles and local listings

## Writing a useful vendor profile

A clear profile helps customers quickly understand what a stall sells and where
to find it. Include:

- The vendor's chosen business or stall name.
- The main products or service, using familiar product names.
- The neighborhood, market, landmark, or service area supplied by the vendor.
- A simple way to contact or place an order, only if the vendor supplies it.
- One truthful point of difference, only when the vendor has provided it.

Keep the first sentence direct. Use short sentences and the language customers
use. Avoid inventing awards, years in business, delivery, opening hours, prices,
certifications, customer reviews, or claims such as "best in town." If a detail
is missing, omit it or ask the vendor.

Example structure:

> **[Business name] — [main product] in [neighborhood]**
> [Business name] offers [vendor-provided products] in [vendor-provided area].
> Visit the stall or contact us at [vendor-provided contact].

The bracketed text is a placeholder, not a factual claim.

## Online listing checklist

When creating or updating a listing on a platform the vendor chooses:

1. Use the same business name, phone number, and area consistently.
2. Select the closest accurate business category; do not choose a category
   merely because it attracts more searches.
3. Add a concise description, current hours, and product photos that the vendor
   has permission to use.
4. Confirm the map pin and service area with the vendor before publishing.
5. Keep prices, availability, and contact details current.
6. Ask real customers for honest feedback; never create fake reviews.
7. Review the platform's current rules and privacy settings before publishing.

Platform features, verification requirements, fees, and eligibility can change.
Check the chosen platform's current official guidance instead of assuming a
feature is available.

## Local discoverability

- Mention the actual neighborhood and nearby market or landmark in the profile.
- Use the words customers naturally use for the products.
- Share the listing link on the vendor's own WhatsApp status, signboard, or
  receipt when appropriate.
- Use clear, recent photos of the real stall and products.
- Ask satisfied customers to share the listing or leave an honest review.
- Measure which actions bring inquiries; do not assume search ranking or demand.
