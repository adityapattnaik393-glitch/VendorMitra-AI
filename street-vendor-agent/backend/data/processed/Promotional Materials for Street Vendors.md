# Promotional materials for street vendors

## WhatsApp message format

Use one short greeting, the vendor-confirmed stall name and products, the
vendor-confirmed location or hours, and a clear next step. Send promotional
messages only to people who have agreed to receive them.

> Namaste! [Business name] has [vendor-provided products] at [vendor-provided
> location]. [Vendor-confirmed offer, if any.] Visit us or contact [vendor
> contact]. Reply STOP if you no longer want updates.

Do not invent an offer, contact number, opening time, delivery option, or
product availability.

## Signboard

Make the business name and main product readable from a short distance. Add
only a confirmed location cue or contact detail. Keep the text brief and use
high contrast.

## QR-payment poster

Tell customers which payment method the vendor actually accepts and where to
scan the vendor's genuine payment QR code. Never generate, redraw, or replace a
payment QR code from text; the vendor must add the code supplied by their
payment provider.

> [Business name] | [Products] | Scan the vendor's QR code to pay
>
> **[Place the verified payment-provider QR code here]**

Use placeholders until the vendor supplies and verifies the details. Do not
promise rewards, refunds, discounts, or payment success unless the relevant
provider has confirmed them.
