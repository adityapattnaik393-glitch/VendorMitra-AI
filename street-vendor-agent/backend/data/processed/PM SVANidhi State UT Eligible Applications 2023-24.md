# PM SVANidhi eligible applications: state and UT overview for 2023-24

Source date: data as of 2024-01-30; Rajya Sabha answer dated 2024-02-08

This historical source reports state/UT-wise eligible applications for the
Prime Minister Street Vendors Atma Nirbhar Nidhi (PM SVANidhi) scheme during
2023-24. The reported total was 39,74,546 eligible applications as of 30
January 2024. The page lists Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh,
Gujarat, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal, Haryana, Delhi, and Andhra Pradesh
as the top ten State/UTs by eligible application count.

This is an aggregate count of applications, not a list of individual vendors,
not a current count, and not proof that a particular vendor is eligible or has
received a loan. Do not use the aggregate figures to decide an individual's
eligibility or infer local demand.

https://artefacts.data.gov.in/state-ut-wise-eligible-application-for-prime-minister-street-vendors-atma-nirbhar-nidhi-pm-svanidhi-scheme-during-2023-24/

The page attributes the figures to Rajya Sabha Session 263, Unstarred Question
No. 737, answered on 8 February 2024, Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.
It links the underlying resource titled "State/UT-wise Details of
Beneficiaries ... as on 30-01-2024" on data.gov.in.

For current scheme rules, application status, or an individual's eligibility,
check the latest official PM SVANidhi and Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs
guidance. This page's figures are historical, not real-time data.
