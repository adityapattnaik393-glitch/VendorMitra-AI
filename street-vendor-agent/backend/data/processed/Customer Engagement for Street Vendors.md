# Customer engagement for street vendors

## Build trust and repeat visits

- Greet customers politely and explain products clearly.
- Display prices and quantities so customers can make informed choices.
- Ask permission before adding a customer to a messaging list or sending
  promotional messages.
- Give customers a simple way to contact the stall, only if the vendor provides
  one and wants it shared.
- Invite honest feedback and respond calmly to concerns.
- Keep a simple sales or stock record to learn which products customers ask for.

## Useful messages

Keep a message short, specific, and truthful. Include the stall name, product,
area, and contact or opening information only when those details are confirmed.
Avoid repeated unsolicited messages, false scarcity, fake testimonials, or
unverified health and quality claims.

## Simple weekly review

At the end of each week, note:

- Which products sold and which remained.
- Common customer questions or requests.
- Which customer-approved messages led to inquiries.
- Any changes needed to stock, display, or opening information.

Use the vendor's own records to decide what to try next. Do not infer demand or
customer preferences from general advice alone.
